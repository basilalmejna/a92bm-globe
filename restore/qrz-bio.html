Welcome & 73!

Thank you for visiting my page and for the QSO. I'm Basil, operating from Diyar Al Muharraq, Bahrain, and on the air since April 2025. What drew me to amateur radio is the meeting point of science, technology and people — the idea that a signal leaving my antenna can reach someone on the other side of the world, and that every contact begins a small conversation and, often, a new friendship.

What I enjoy most is real conversation. On HF my focus is SSB voice — not just a quick signal report, but a real chat: sharing stories and learning about the other operator’s station, country and life, anywhere from 40 to 10 m. When conditions are weak I switch to FT8, but nothing beats a good voice QSO. Closer to home, 2 m is my playground in every mode — FM, digital voice and SSB — while on UHF (70 cm) I spend most of my time on D-STAR, DMR and System Fusion (C4FM), staying in touch with friends across the Gulf.

My special interest is APRS — position tracking, messaging and beaconing — and LoRa, with its long-range, low-power potential for digital links. I am equally drawn to the technical side of the hobby: software-defined radio, networked station control, digital modes and the computers and software that tie a modern station together. I like to understand how each piece works, not just that it works.

Away from the radio desk, I love the outdoors — mainly hiking, and camping under the open sky. When I can, I take a light radio with me and enjoy operating from the field.

My intention is simple: keep learning, keep experimenting, and give back — share what I learn with newcomers the way others generously shared with me, and help the amateur radio community in the Gulf keep growing. If you share any of these interests, I would love to hear from you.

Hope to hear you on the bands. If we worked, please confirm on QRZ.
